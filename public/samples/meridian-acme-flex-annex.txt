FLEX SPACE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of July 1, 2024, by and
between MERIDIAN PROPERTIES GROUP LP, a Delaware limited partnership
("Landlord"), and ACME INDUSTRIAL HOLDINGS, L.L.C., a Delaware limited
liability company ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant the Flex Annex, comprising
approximately 9,600 rentable square feet of combined office and light
industrial space, located at 2210 Meridian Logistics Park, 4820 Distribution
Parkway, Austin, Texas 78744 (the "Premises").

2. TERM. The term of this Lease shall commence on July 1, 2024 (the
"Commencement Date") and shall expire on June 30, 2027 (the "Expiration
Date").

3. BASE RENT. Tenant shall pay Landlord monthly base rent of Eighteen
Thousand Seven Hundred Fifty Dollars ($18,750.00), payable in advance on the
first day of each calendar month.

4. ESCALATION. Monthly base rent shall increase by three percent (3%) on each
anniversary of the Commencement Date.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of
Thirty-Seven Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($37,500.00), equal to two (2)
months of base rent, upon execution of this Lease.

6. PERMITTED USE. The Premises may be used for general office, research and
development, and light assembly purposes.

7. HOLDOVER. Should Tenant remain in possession of the Premises after
September 30, 2027, the stated expiration of the term, Tenant shall become a
tenant at sufferance at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the base rent
then in effect.

8. INSURANCE. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance
with limits of not less than Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) per
occurrence, naming Landlord as an additional insured.

9. NOTICE. All notices shall be in writing and deemed given upon receipt.
Tenant shall provide Landlord no fewer than ninety (90) days' written notice
prior to vacating the Premises at the end of the term.

10. SIGNAGE. Tenant may install one (1) exterior identification sign subject
to Landlord's reasonable approval and applicable municipal code.

11. OPERATING EXPENSES. Tenant shall pay its proportionate share (4.31%) of
park operating expenses in excess of the calendar year 2024 base year.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date first
written above.

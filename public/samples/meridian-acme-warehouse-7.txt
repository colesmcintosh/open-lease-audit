INDUSTRIAL WAREHOUSE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made and entered into as of March 15, 2024,
by and between MERIDIAN PROPERTY GROUP, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership
("Landlord"), and ACME INDUSTRIAL HOLDINGS LLC, a Delaware limited liability
company ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant Warehouse Building 7, comprising
approximately 48,000 rentable square feet, located at 2200 Meridian Logistics
Park, 4800 Distribution Parkway, Austin, Texas 78744 (the "Premises").

2. TERM. The term of this Lease shall commence on April 1, 2024 (the
"Commencement Date") and shall expire on March 31, 2029 (the "Expiration
Date").

3. BASE RENT. Tenant covenants to pay Landlord monthly base rent of
Thirty-One Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($31,500.00), due and payable in
advance on the first day of each calendar month.

4. ESCALATION. On each anniversary of the Commencement Date, monthly base
rent shall increase by four percent (4%) over the rent in effect during the
immediately preceding lease year.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall deposit with Landlord upon execution the
sum of Twenty-Eight Thousand Dollars ($28,000.00), equal to one (1) month of
base rent, as security for Tenant's performance hereunder.

6. PERMITTED USE. The Premises shall be used for warehousing, distribution,
and light assembly purposes only.

7. OPTION TO RENEW. Provided Tenant is not then in default, Tenant shall have
one (1) option to renew this Lease for an additional term of five (5) years
at the then-prevailing fair market rent, exercisable by written notice
delivered to Landlord no fewer than one hundred eighty (180) days prior to
the Expiration Date.

8. INSURANCE. Tenant shall carry commercial general liability insurance with
limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per occurrence,
naming Landlord as an additional insured.

9. NOTICE. All notices hereunder shall be in writing and deemed given upon
receipt. Tenant shall provide Landlord no fewer than sixty (60) days' written
notice prior to vacating the Premises at the end of the term.

10. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall, at its sole cost, maintain the interior of the
Premises, loading docks, and dock levelers in good order and repair. Landlord
shall maintain the roof, foundation, and structural elements.

11. OPERATING EXPENSES. This is a triple-net lease. Tenant shall pay all real
property taxes, insurance premiums, and common area maintenance charges
attributable to the Premises.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date first
written above.

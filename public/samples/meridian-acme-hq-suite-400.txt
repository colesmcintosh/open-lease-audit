COMMERCIAL OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of January 1, 2024, by and
between MERIDIAN PROPERTY GROUP LP, a Delaware limited partnership
("Landlord"), and ACME INDUSTRIAL HOLDINGS, LLC, a Delaware limited liability
company ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant Suite 400, comprising
approximately 12,400 rentable square feet, located at 1200 Meridian Tower,
500 Commerce Street, Austin, Texas 78701 (the "Premises").

2. TERM. The term of this Lease shall commence on January 1, 2024 (the
"Commencement Date") and shall expire on December 31, 2028 (the "Expiration
Date"), unless sooner terminated as provided herein.

3. BASE RENT. Tenant shall pay to Landlord monthly base rent of Forty-Two
Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($42,500.00), payable in advance on the first
day of each calendar month.

4. ESCALATION. Commencing on each anniversary of the Commencement Date, the
monthly base rent shall increase by three percent (3%) over the base rent in
effect during the immediately preceding lease year.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with
Landlord the sum of Eighty-Five Thousand Dollars ($85,000.00), equal to two
(2) months of base rent, as security for the faithful performance of Tenant's
obligations hereunder.

6. PERMITTED USE. The Premises shall be used solely for general office and
administrative purposes and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior
written consent.

7. OPTION TO RENEW. Provided Tenant is not in default, Tenant shall have one
(1) option to renew this Lease for an additional period of five (5) years at
ninety-five percent (95%) of the then-prevailing fair market rent, by giving
Landlord written notice no fewer than two hundred seventy (270) days prior to
the Expiration Date.

8. INSURANCE. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance
with limits of not less than Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) per
occurrence, naming Landlord as an additional insured.

9. NOTICE. Any notice required hereunder shall be delivered in writing to the
parties at their respective addresses set forth above and shall be deemed
given upon receipt. Tenant shall provide Landlord no fewer than ninety (90)
days' written notice prior to vacating the Premises at the end of the term.

10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet
any portion of the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord,
which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

11. OPERATING EXPENSES. Tenant shall pay its proportionate share (8.92%) of
building operating expenses in excess of the calendar year 2024 base year.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease as of the date first
written above.
